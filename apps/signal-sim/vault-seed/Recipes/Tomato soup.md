# Tomato soup

Serves 4.

- 1 kg ripe tomatoes (or 2 cans)
- 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, a pinch of sugar
- Fresh basil

1. Soften the onion and garlic in olive oil.
2. Add the tomatoes and stock, simmer 20 minutes.
3. Blend, season, and serve with basil and a spoon of cream.
